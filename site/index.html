How a match works

01 Choose 8 from 23

Build a deck from the cards you can play now.

02 Hold 4 cards in hand

Use one card. The next one joins your hand.

3

03 Spend elixir

Cards have a cost. Elixir refills to 10.

04 Take the towers

Destroy more towers by 3 minutes. Ties go to sudden death, then a tower-health tiebreak at 5 minutes. Take the centre tower to win immediately.
