# Aether Well

**Identity:** Aether Well, a copper-and-rootwood emberseed well beside the woodland river.

**Functional role:** Stationary resource building that gathers emberseed for the river workshop.

## Lore

At dusk, the Aether Well’s copper scoop gathers glowing emberseed from the quiet river water.

Workshop keepers wind its rootwood crank to lift the seeds into stoppered flasks for the nearby repair shed.
