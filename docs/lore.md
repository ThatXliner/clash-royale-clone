# Moss & Ember: world and card stories

Moss & Ember is a valley of root-bridged woodland, river workshops, and copper roofs warmed by forge light. When emberseeds began glowing along forgotten paths, the valley’s makers and wanderers set out to trace where those paths led.

## The collection

### Coilblade

A clockwork duelist was assembled from a cracked kiln bell and copper from the old riverworks. At dusk, its blade catches the forge light like a thin line of sunrise.

### Mossguard

This grove keeper wears a shield cut from an old bough, with leaves pressed into the grain. Each leaf marks a trail the woodland guides promised to keep clear.

### Copperskiff

The little airship began as a copper kettle patched into a hull beside the mill. Its canvas sail still carries the scent of river rain.

### Skymaul

A stone wanderer drifted down from the cloud shelf, carrying a hammer left behind by the first valley smith. Copper bands around its arms keep little chips of rock from tumbling loose.

### Brassbolt

The ranger made a crossbow from a brass hinge and a piece of bell metal. Amber fletching on each bolt points home to the lanterns of the foundry.

### Reedsting

A marsh pathfinder ties reeds into a hood and the cords of each arrow. Small stitched marks on the lining count every crossing through the river grass.

### Briar Band

The runners and darters met in a thorn maze and chose the same leaf mark for their masks. They leave chalk arrows on the stumps so the next scout can find the way out.

### Cinderlings

Three ember sprouts woke beneath a fallen lantern in a bed of ash. They gather sparks in nutshell caps and leave tiny lights along the stones.

### Stonehauler

This broad pack carrier is built around a core of quarry stone, wrapped in weathered rootwood. Copper bells on its straps ring out when it reaches the river workshops.
