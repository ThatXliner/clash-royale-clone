# Moss & Ember brand

Moss & Ember is an original identity for this project. The mark joins a jade
leaf and an ember flame around a pale seed: two distinct forces held in one
measured arena. It does not use the comparison game's logo, card names, or
visual assets.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#101a22` | Primary dark surface and high-contrast text. |
| Jade | `#9bbb76` | Moss accent, outlines, and selected states. |
| Ember | `#f2a25e` | Warm accent, active details, and highlights. |
| Paper | `#e9e2cd` | Light type, marks, and readable surface contrast. |

Use ink as the main background with paper for primary text. Reserve jade and
ember for short accents so they stay distinct. Do not recolor one team as
another product's recognizable faction or copy a third-party wordmark.

## Type and use

The wordmark uses a system serif; interface and supporting copy use a neutral
system sans serif. The SVGs contain no linked fonts, scripts, raster effects,
or external resources, so they remain usable in README and Pages previews.
Keep the mark's aspect ratio, preserve clear space around it, and use the
complete banner rather than cropping its wordmark.
