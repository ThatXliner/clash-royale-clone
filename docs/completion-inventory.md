## Remaining release gaps

- Full pre-Evolution behavior remains open across the 109-card target. The pinned source ledger marks unverified mechanics as QUESTIONS; an absent or normalized field is not evidence that the historical mechanic does not exist.
- The frozen 18-choice map still has no full historical execution or parity claims. Its mappings and tests are useful evidence joins, not a substitute for shared `GameSession` execution of each card.
- Kilnkeeper's accepted core covers ordinary ranged splash, and CARDS-019H1 accepts bounded shared homing target-following at local commit `74cd3a8`; historical calibration remains QUESTION in H2. The live Chrome capture shows generic card details with no portrait or lore. CARDS-019D remains FAIL while its combat-stat uncertainty labels are repaired, and CARDS-019I card/arena integration remains BUILDING; neither presentation review is accepted.
- Endurance applies to candidate `9f640113` and the default-eight workload. It does not cover the changed `dbd7afe4` runtime, every editable card, every deck, or the 109-card roster.
- The latest public site is the `dbd7afe4` Pages deployment. It exposes 20 collection choices, including the newly admitted Kilnkeeper through its generic fallback, with no portrait or lore. The accepted local H1 runtime integration and any later art or UI work are not part of that deployment until a matching workflow run and live verification succeed.
