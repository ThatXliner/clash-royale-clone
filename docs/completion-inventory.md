## Remaining release gaps

- Full pre-Evolution behavior remains open across the 109-card target. The source ledger marks unverified mechanics as QUESTIONS; an absent or normalized field is not proof that a historical mechanic does not exist.
- The frozen 18-choice map has no full historical executions or parity claims. Its mappings are evidence joins, not a substitute for shared `GameSession` execution of each card. A larger collection is not proof of complete card behavior.
- H1 and CARDS-019I acceptance cover their bounded runtime/presentation paths. Historical homing calibration remains QUESTION in H2. Stormkite's chain and policy estimates likewise retain their QUESTION labels.
- The older `9f64011378d3ce1c6281d9292950a7b52f11fd2c` default-eight endurance, R631's R4 default-eight 18,000-tick run, R637's R7 seed-nine custom run, and the f949 live smoke are distinct evidence. None establishes every-deck endurance or a fresh full default-eight qualification on f949.
- Low-confidence source values remain questions rather than authoritative mismatch gates. See the [historical source ledger](../reference/stats/SOURCES.md). No full repository-suite or complete 109-card execution claim follows from the incremental publication checks.
