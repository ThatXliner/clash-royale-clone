How to play

  1. 01 Build a deck of eight cards. Choose any eight from the collection. Open a card to see what it does.

  2. 02 Play from a four-card hand. Select a card, then click or tap a spot on your half of the field. The next card slides in.

  3. 3

    03 Spend elixir. The copper tag on each card is its cost. Elixir refills on its own, up to 10.

  4. 04 Take the towers. Knock down towers to earn crowns. More crowns after three minutes wins, and a tie goes to overtime.
