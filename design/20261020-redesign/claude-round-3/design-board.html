About this board

Selected direction from art-direction.md: a tactile woodland, river-workshop, copper and emberseed diorama. The board shows the public landing page, the deck/collection/card-info screen and the battle HUD at 390×844 and 1440×933, plus the cast's silhouettes at 13 px and static motion keys. All art is original inline SVG/CSS; nothing from reference/ was used. Lore is quoted verbatim from src/ui/card-lore.js. No fonts are loaded; system stacks only. The page makes no network requests.

Palette & type

The existing brand and combat tokens are kept unchanged; new tokens fill the gaps. The river is deliberately desaturated so it never reads as the teal team.

Cast: identity, dominant read, 13 px test

The vector stand-ins follow the existing painted portraits (atlas cells 0–2) and the lore. Astra's low-poly renders replace them; see character-to-3D-handoff.md. The green strip shows each unit at its phone arena size of about 13 px for both teams, in grayscale and as a black silhouette. That's the test the final sprites must pass on the real arena surface.

Coilblade

Thin upright fencer + long bright crescent blade

A clockwork duelist was assembled from a cracked kiln bell and copper from the old riverworks. At dusk, its blade catches the forge light like a thin line of sunrise.

  • Kept from portrait: lean copper limbs, dome head with one round eye, green scarf, long curved saber.
  • Additions: exposed waist coil and a small wind key (lore/name-derived, optional).

Mossguard

Round leaf dome on top + tall shield leading

This grove keeper wears a shield cut from an old bough, with leaves pressed into the grain. Each leaf marks a trail the woodland guides promised to keep clear.

  • Kept from portrait: leaf mantle, carved smiling mask with sprout, tall wooden shield with a large leaf relief, no weapon.
  • Team color lives on the shield rim (existing team zone = shield).

Copperskiff

Pale canvas lens floating over a small copper boat

The little airship began as a copper kettle patched into a hull beside the mill. Its canvas sail still carries the scent of river rain.

  • Kept from portrait: cream envelope, copper strap cage, leaf flag, porthole gondola, stern prop (no pilot, no top rotor).
  • Addition: short kettle-spout prow where the ember shot leaves (lore-derived, optional).
