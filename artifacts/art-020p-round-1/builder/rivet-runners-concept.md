# Rivet Runners

**Runtime future ID:** `rivet-runners`  
**Functional role:** Two fast ground melee river-otter couriers who fight as a pair with chunky rootwood mallets.  
**Stage:** Collection portrait and original lore only; combat sprites and gameplay admission are separate work.

## Lore

The Rivet Runners carry copper repair kits between river mills, using paired rootwood mallets to clear fallen timber. When emberseed alarms sound, they abandon their deliveries and race together to defend the workshop bridges.

## Portrait direction

Two shoulder-to-shoulder adult anthropomorphic river otters, each with a large readable face, practical copper-fastened moss-teal work vest, and a clear chunky rootwood mallet. Warm river-mill light, glowing emberseed signal lamps, and a softly painted workshop background.
