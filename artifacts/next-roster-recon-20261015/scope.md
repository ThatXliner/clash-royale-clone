## Result and next safe step

No lead above meets the requested bar for a new positive historical mechanical mapping: the test-only comparison key is explicit, but the majority of candidate signatures remain QUESTION-backed, and the special mechanics are not closed by the normalized card rows. This conclusion is about missing positive source closure, not about branding-ID equality/inequality or a presumed lack of mechanics.

Before fetching any raw rows, intentionally pin the chosen original-ID → historical-card-key relation in the **test-only comparison layer** (the existing pattern is an explicit `EXPECTED` map plus `createReferenceParityEvaluator`, as in `tests/cards-basic-melee.test.js:21-76`). Keep the shipped `src/content/cards/` identity original. The binding should name the exact target key and any linked character/projectile/spawn selectors; only after that should a bounded extractor record complete ordered raw rows, upstream commit, file SHA-256, selector results, and per-field dispositions. For the minimal first source-closure attempt, Wayfinder/Knight avoids projectile and spawn joins, but still needs nine low-confidence base-field corroborations and sourced retarget behavior.
