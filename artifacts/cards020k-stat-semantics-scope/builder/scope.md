## Raw values and confidence boundary

The card `26000043` maps to troop record `AngryBarbarians`, which summons two `AngryBarbarian` characters. The raw troop row has `summon_deploy_delay=100`; the raw character row has `deploy_delay=400`, `walking_speed_tweak_percentage=-20`, `collision_radius=500`, and `mass=4`. The same four character values occur on the pinned `Barbarian` and `RageBarbarian` sibling rows. This repetition shows the fields are shared by related character records; it does not define their effects. Raw times are milliseconds and distances are thousandths of a tile per the [local source ledger](../../../reference/stats/SOURCES.md#L25-L27). The datamine ledger rates these character constants as a source snapshot, not a second-source-confirmed runtime interpretation.

**020K implication:** a profile-only admission cannot honestly claim the full ordinary two-member melee role while omitting these source fields. The evidence does not identify a unique card-specific ability subsystem; the immediate gate is shared character-stat semantics and runtime representation.
