## Finding

Consequently there are no observed timecodes, no measured formation/order, and no frame-rate evidence. ## Search boundary

The search was limited to official Supercell release-note pages and indexed videos from the Clash Royale publisher channel in the December 2022–February 2023 window. Queries combined the target names (“Bats”, “Minion Horde”) with official channel, gameplay, TV Royale, season, and update terms. Indexed descriptions and the linked official pages were checked; items whose text described announcements, comments, or unrelated challenges were excluded. This was not an exhaustive review of every frame in every official upload, and it did not search or promote third-party creator footage. That boundary is why the result is “no qualifying candidate identified,” not “no such scene exists.”
